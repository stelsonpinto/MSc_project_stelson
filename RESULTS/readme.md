## RESULTS

This directory contains the primary outputs generated by the analysis pipeline.

### RAW_GWAS
Summary tables of the analysed GWAS loci. Each row represents one susceptibility locus together with its lead variant, genomic coordinates and associated GWAS summary statistics used for downstream fine-mapping and colocalisation analyses.

### RAW_FINE_MAPPING
Bayesian fine-mapping (SuSiE) outputs, including posterior inclusion probabilities and credible sets.

### RAW_COLOC
Bayesian colocalisation (coloc.susie) summary statistics and posterior probabilities.

Publication-quality figures and supplementary tables are provided in the accompanying dissertation and are not duplicated here.
